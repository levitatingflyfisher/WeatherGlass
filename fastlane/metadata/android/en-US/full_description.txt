Read the sky, tell no one.

WeatherGlass rounds your coordinates BEFORE they leave the device, uses one open keyless weather source (Open-Meteo), and shows you the exact request it made so you can check the claim yourself.

• Current conditions, hourly and daily forecast
• Coordinates rounded before the request — a tested invariant, not a promise
• A "What leaves your device" screen that spells out every byte
• No API key, no account, no ad network, no analytics

The one network call this app makes is the forecast, and you can read it.

Free and open source, MIT licensed.
Source: https://github.com/levitatingflyfisher/WeatherGlass
